
## 1. Root-CA: Schlüssel und selbstsigniertes Zertifikat (30 Tage; im Echtbetrieb länger und Schlüssel geschützt)
$ openssl req -x509 -newkey rsa:2048 -nodes -keyout ca.key -out ca.pem -days 30 -subj "/O=Lab/CN=Lab Root CA"
   subject=O = Lab, CN = Lab Root CA
   issuer=O = Lab, CN = Lab Root CA
   notBefore=Nov 19 19:19:41 2026 GMT
   notAfter=Dec 19 19:19:41 2026 GMT
   X509v3 Basic Constraints: critical
       CA:TRUE

## 4. Kette prüfen
$ openssl verify -CAfile ca.pem server.crt
server.crt: OK
$ openssl x509 -in server.crt -noout -checkhost shop.lab.test
Hostname shop.lab.test does match certificate
$ openssl x509 -in server.crt -noout -checkhost www.example.org
Hostname www.example.org does NOT match certificate
$ openssl x509 -in server.crt -noout -checkend 86400
Certificate will not expire
$ openssl x509 -in server.crt -noout -checkend 3888000
Certificate will expire
